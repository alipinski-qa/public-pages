Card metadata injection - og:title & og:description

Sample Company QA harness page. Link to this page from a shared Confluence page and view it as a card.

  • og:title carries an <img onerror> HTML-injection payload.
  • og:description carries an attribute/tag breakout ("><svg onload>).
